# Kinetic title

Use for an identity card, a chapter marker or the opening of a short film.

## Shape

One scene, 3-5 seconds. A quiet background, an eyebrow label, a large wordmark,
a rule beneath it, and one supporting line.

## Beats

1. Build: the rule draws, the eyebrow slides in, then the wordmark arrives one
   letter at a time with a slight 3D rotation. Overlap the entrances.
2. Breathe: the wordmark drifts a few pixels horizontally while the background
   grid shifts in the opposite direction.
3. Resolve: a second accent line extends under the supporting copy, or a small
   mono index fades in at the bottom edge.

## Notes

- Letter stagger between 0.03s and 0.06s. Longer reads as separate words.
- Keep the rule and the wordmark on the same left edge.
- Do not add particles or a glow bloom; the type is the event.
