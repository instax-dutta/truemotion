# Data story

Use when one number or one relationship carries the message.

## Shape

One or two scenes. One large figure, one label that tells the viewer what to
notice, and a single supporting line. No chart junk.

## Beats

1. Build: the label arrives first, then the figure counts up on a proxy tween.
   Use tabular figures so the digits do not jitter.
2. Breathe: a thin rule or bar grows to the value while the figure holds.
3. Resolve: the supporting line appears and everything settles.

## Notes

- Round the counter to a sensible unit; rapidly changing small digits are hard
  to read.
- If showing a comparison, use two panels and an explicit seam rather than a
  crowded chart.
- Give the number a role and a verb. A number that simply fades in is not a
  data story.
